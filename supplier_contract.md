# Master Supplier Agreement (Template)
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Scope of Agreement
This agreement governs the supply of goods between SupplySense Manufacturing Group
("the Company") and any registered supplier ("the Supplier"). It applies to all
purchase orders issued under the Company's procurement system.

## Section 2: Delivery Obligations
2.1 The Supplier shall deliver goods within the lead time specified on each purchase
order. The standard acceptable delivery window is the quoted lead time plus a
grace period of 2 calendar days.

2.2 A delivery is considered "late" if goods arrive more than 2 days after the
agreed delivery date stated on the purchase order.

2.3 The Supplier must notify the Company's procurement team at least 3 business
days in advance of any anticipated delay.

## Section 3: Late Delivery Penalties
3.1 For each late delivery, a penalty of 0.5% of the order value shall be applied
per day of delay, up to a maximum of 10% of the total order value.

3.2 If a Supplier records more than 15% of orders as delayed within a rolling
90-day period, the Supplier shall automatically be flagged for a **Performance
Review** (see Section 4).

3.3 Repeated late delivery — defined as three or more late deliveries within any
rolling 60-day window — may result in suspension of new purchase orders until a
corrective action plan is submitted and approved.

## Section 4: Performance Review
4.1 A Performance Review is triggered when a Supplier's on-time delivery rate
falls below 85% over a rolling quarter, or when flagged under Section 3.2.

4.2 The review evaluates: on-time delivery rate, quality score, responsiveness
to communication, and root cause of delays.

4.3 Following a Performance Review, the Company may: (a) continue the
relationship with a corrective action plan, (b) reduce order volume allocated
to the Supplier, or (c) terminate the agreement with 30 days' written notice.

## Section 5: Quality Requirements
5.1 All goods must meet the quality specifications stated in the applicable
Purchase Order and pass incoming inspection.

5.2 A defect rate above 5% in any shipment gives the Company the right to
reject the shipment in full and request replacement at the Supplier's cost.

## Section 6: Termination
6.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days' written notice.

6.2 The Company may terminate immediately, without notice, in cases of fraud,
repeated safety violations, or breach of confidentiality obligations.

## Section 7: Confidentiality
7.1 Both parties agree to keep pricing, forecasts, and proprietary specifications
confidential and not disclose them to third parties without written consent.
