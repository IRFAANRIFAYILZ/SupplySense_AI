# Purchase Order Guidelines
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Required Fields
1.1 Every purchase order must include: supplier ID, product ID(s), quantity,
unit cost, requested delivery date, priority level, and the responsible
procurement analyst's name.

## Section 2: Priority Levels
2.1 Orders may be marked Standard, High, or Urgent.
   - Standard: routine replenishment, standard lead time applies.
   - High: expedited handling requested; supplier should prioritize picking
     and shipping, 1-day grace period on delivery.
   - Urgent: production-critical shortage; zero grace period, and the
     supplier must confirm receipt of the order within 4 business hours.

## Section 2: Minimum Order Quantities
2.2 Orders below a supplier's stated Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) may be
consolidated with other pending orders to the same supplier where possible,
to avoid MOQ surcharges.

## Section 3: Amendments and Cancellations
3.1 A purchase order may be amended (quantity or delivery date) up to 48 hours
after issuance without penalty, provided the supplier has not yet begun
fulfillment.

3.2 Cancellations after the supplier has begun fulfillment may incur a
cancellation fee of up to 15% of order value, subject to negotiation.

## Section 4: Order Matching and Receiving
4.1 Upon receipt, goods are matched against the purchase order (quantity,
product ID, and quality spec) before being logged into inventory. Discrepancies
must be reported within 48 hours of receipt.

## Section 5: Record Retention
5.1 Purchase orders and associated delivery records must be retained for a
minimum of 3 years for audit purposes.
