# Inventory Management Policy
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Reorder Point Methodology
1.1 Every product has a defined Reorder Level, calculated as approximately
15–35% of average monthly demand, adjusted for the supplier's average lead
time and demand variability.

1.2 When current stock falls below the Reorder Level, the product is flagged
as "Low Stock" and a replenishment purchase order should be initiated within
2 business days.

## Section 2: Minimum Inventory Levels
2.1 The Company's minimum acceptable inventory level for any active product is
the Reorder Level itself. Stock is not permitted to be allowed to reach zero
for products with active monthly demand, except for discontinued items.

2.2 Products flagged Low Stock for more than 10 consecutive business days
without a corresponding purchase order must be escalated to the Inventory
Manager.

## Section 3: Safety Stock for Critical Categories
3.1 Products in the "Electronics" and "Machinery Parts" categories, which
have longer average lead times, should maintain a safety stock buffer of an
additional 10% above the standard Reorder Level.

## Section 4: Excess Inventory
4.1 Inventory exceeding 4x the Reorder Level for more than 60 days is
classified as Excess Inventory and reviewed for potential overstock write-down
or demand forecast correction.

## Section 5: Cycle Counting
5.1 High-value products (unit cost above $200) are cycle-counted monthly.
All other products are cycle-counted quarterly.

## Section 6: Inventory Valuation
6.1 Inventory value is calculated as current stock multiplied by unit cost,
and is reported at the category level in the Executive Dashboard.
