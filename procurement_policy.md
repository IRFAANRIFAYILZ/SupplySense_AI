# Procurement Policy
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Purpose
This policy establishes the rules for how the Company sources, evaluates, and
purchases goods and services from external suppliers.

## Section 2: Supplier Onboarding
2.1 New suppliers must complete a Supplier Registration Form and pass an initial
risk assessment before being added to the Approved Supplier List.

2.2 Suppliers handling categories classified as "Critical" (Electronics,
Machinery Parts) require a facility audit before approval.

## Section 3: Purchase Order Authorization Limits
3.1 Purchase orders under $10,000 may be approved by a Procurement Analyst.

3.2 Purchase orders between $10,000 and $100,000 require approval from a
Procurement Manager.

3.3 Purchase orders above $100,000 require approval from the Director of
Procurement and Finance sign-off.

## Section 4: Competitive Bidding
4.1 Any new sourcing arrangement expected to exceed $50,000 in annual spend must
go through a competitive bidding process with at least three quotes.

4.2 Sole-sourcing (using a single supplier without competitive bids) is only
permitted for proprietary components with no viable alternative supplier, and
must be documented with a Sole Source Justification form.

## Section 5: Supplier Diversification
5.1 To reduce concentration risk, no single supplier should account for more
than 40% of total spend within a product category, where alternative suppliers
exist.

5.2 Categories with fewer than 2 active approved suppliers are flagged as
"Single Point of Failure" categories and prioritized for supplier diversification
efforts.

## Section 6: Cost Review
6.1 Supplier pricing (reflected in the cost index) is reviewed semi-annually.
A cost index more than 20% above the category average requires justification
or renegotiation.

## Section 7: Emergency Procurement
7.1 In the event of a supply disruption affecting production, Emergency
Procurement procedures allow expedited approval (single manager sign-off,
regardless of order value) for a maximum of 15 days, after which normal
approval limits resume.
