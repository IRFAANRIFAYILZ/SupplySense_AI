# Supplier Evaluation Guidelines
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Purpose
These guidelines define how supplier performance is scored and how frequently
suppliers must be evaluated.

## Section 2: Evaluation Frequency
2.1 All active suppliers are formally evaluated quarterly.

2.2 Suppliers under an active Performance Review (see Master Supplier
Agreement, Section 4) are evaluated monthly until performance is restored
above the review threshold.

## Section 3: Scoring Dimensions
3.1 Supplier evaluation considers four weighted dimensions:
   - On-Time Delivery Rate — 40% weight
   - Quality Score — 30% weight
   - Cost Competitiveness (relative to category average) — 20% weight
   - Responsiveness / Communication — 10% weight

## Section 4: Risk Classification Requirements
4.1 A supplier is classified as HIGH risk when on-time delivery falls below
70% AND at least one additional dimension (quality or cost) is also below
the acceptable threshold in the same evaluation period.

4.2 A supplier is classified as MEDIUM risk when on-time delivery is between
70–90%, or when a single dimension is significantly underperforming while
others remain acceptable.

4.3 A supplier is classified as LOW risk when on-time delivery exceeds 90%
and quality score exceeds 85%.

## Section 5: Minimum Requirements to Remain an Approved Supplier
5.1 Suppliers must maintain a minimum quality score of 70 and an on-time
delivery rate of at least 60% to remain on the Approved Supplier List without
a corrective action plan.

5.2 Any supplier falling below these minimums for two consecutive quarters is
automatically escalated for contract review and potential removal from the
Approved Supplier List.

## Section 6: New Supplier Probation
6.1 Newly onboarded suppliers are placed on a 90-day probationary evaluation
period with weekly delivery performance tracking instead of quarterly.
