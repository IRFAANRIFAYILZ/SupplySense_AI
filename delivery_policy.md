# Delivery & Logistics Policy
**SupplySense Manufacturing Group — Fictional Document for Demonstration Purposes**

## Section 1: Delivery Windows
1.1 Standard priority orders must arrive within the quoted lead time plus a
2-day grace period. High priority orders have a 1-day grace period. Urgent
priority orders have zero grace period — any delay is recorded immediately.

## Section 2: Delay Classification
2.1 Delays are classified by severity:
   - Minor Delay: 1–3 days past the grace period.
   - Moderate Delay: 4–7 days past the grace period.
   - Severe Delay: more than 7 days past the grace period.

2.2 A Severe Delay on an Urgent priority order automatically triggers a
notification to the Supply Chain Risk team and a note on the supplier's
profile.

## Section 3: Seasonal Adjustments
3.1 During the November–January peak season, expected delivery windows may
be extended by mutual agreement, but the Supplier must confirm any adjusted
date in writing at least 5 business days before the original due date.

3.2 Failure to proactively communicate seasonal delays is treated as a
standard late delivery under the Late Delivery Penalties in the Master
Supplier Agreement.

## Section 4: Carrier and Routing
4.1 Suppliers are responsible for selecting a carrier capable of meeting the
agreed delivery window. Repeated delays attributed to carrier choice do not
exempt the Supplier from late delivery penalties.

## Section 5: Proof of Delivery
5.1 Suppliers must provide proof of delivery (tracking confirmation or signed
receipt) within 24 hours of delivery for all orders above $5,000 in value.
